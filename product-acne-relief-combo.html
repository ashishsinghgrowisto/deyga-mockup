Are Deyga products suitable for all skin types?

Yes. Every Deyga formulation is made with gentle, plant-derived ingredients and is suitable for all skin types. For sensitive skin we always recommend a patch test first.

Absolutely — no petroleum jelly, no parabens, no artificial colours and no artificial fragrances. Ever.
